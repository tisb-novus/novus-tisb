About Novus

Bangalore's first student-led robotics fest, built by enthusiasts for enthusiasts.

What is Novus?

Novus is Bangalore's first student-led robotics fest. This non-profit initiative aims to build a community of mechatronics and robotics enthusiasts. The one-day event encourages collaboration, innovation, and learning through interactive challenges, workshops, and exhibitions.

Our Story

NOVUS began with the vision of Aditya Iyer, a passionate student who saw the potential for a robotics community in Bangalore. Starting as a small gathering of innovators, NOVUS has grown into a full-scale festival celebrating technology, creativity, and collaboration.

The Future

The first edition sparked excitement and curiosity, laying the foundation for what would become an annual hub for robotics and innovation. Each year, NOVUS will continue to grow — uniting students, mentors, and industry experts to shape the future of robotics in India.
